# MindSpark Viral Social Media Growth Skill

This skill brings together the outlier social media growth frameworks from `charlie947/social-media-skills` adapted for Sanatan, historical, and mythological short-form video creation in Hindi/Hinglish.

---

## 1. Viral Hook Framework (from `hook-generator`)

A viewer decides whether to scroll or stay within **0 to 3 seconds**.

### The 2-Line High-Stakes Formula
- **Line 1 (The Punchy Fact/Paradox)**: Max 40 characters. No questions, no generic greetings ("Doston", "Aaj dekhenge"). States an unexpected, concrete fact, name, date, or number.
- **Line 2 (The Contrast/Flip)**: Max 40 characters. Contradicts, reframes, or escalates the stakes.

### Proven Hook Archetypes
1. **High-Stakes Paradox**:
   - Line 1: `300 मराठा वीरों ने 4000 मुग़ल रोके!`
   - Line 2: `बिना ढाल, सिर्फ़ अपनी छाती की दीवार से!`
2. **Sacred Mystery**:
   - Line 1: `पुरी मंदिर का ध्वज हवा के विपरीत लहराता है!`
   - Line 2: `विज्ञान के सारे नियम यहाँ शून्य हो जाते हैं!`
3. **The Extreme Vow**:
   - Line 1: `भाई के प्राण बचाने एक कन्या पाताल में उतरी!`
   - Line 2: `काल के सबसे भयानक जीव को अपना वाहन बना लिया!`

## 2. Retention Pacing & Visual Cleanliness

1. **Stamp Cleanliness (Zero Popup Overload)**:
   - **Scene 0 (Hook)**: Only Scene 0 gets the stamp `भाग X`.
   - **Intermediate Scenes (1 to N-2)**: No repetitive stamps! Clean paper presentation.
   - **Final Scene**:
     - Non-final parts: `अगला भाग` (paired with an electrifying Curiosity Question).
     - Final part: `कथा सम्पूर्ण` (paired with deity name + sacred chant).
2. **No Forced Map Scenes**:
   - Maps appear ONLY when a geographic location is an organic, essential beat in the story.
   - Stories without geographical journeys remain focused on character cards, quotes, numbers, and art.

## 3. The Pinned-Comment Algorithm Booster (from `pinned-comment`)

YouTube and Instagram algorithms prioritize videos that generate active comment debates.

### The Formula:
Every video automatically includes a high-engagement **Pinned Comment**:
- **Line 1**: A provocative observation from the video.
- **Line 2**: An open curiosity question for viewers.
- **Line 3**: Call to action asking for their opinion or devotion.

*Example*:
> 📌 *"मंदिर की इस दिव्य घटना के पीछे क्या विज्ञान है या साक्षात भगवान का चमत्कार? कमेंट में अपनी राय ज़रूर बताएं और बोलिए हर हर महादेव! 👇"*
